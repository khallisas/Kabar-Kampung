Kampung Rukun Cilik intervention programme · 2026

Feelings that get named grow into harmony.

Kabar Kampung (Javanese for “village news”) connects teachers and parents through quick notes on small classroom moments and a warm weekly summary, so what children learn at school carries on after the home-time bell. It supports Kampung Rukun Cilik, “the little village of harmony”.

Our promise

Warm, never judging.

These principles come straight from the Kampung Rukun Cilik design and are kept in the digital version too.

01

No grades, no rankings.

Progress is shown as a stage, never a score, and never compared with other children.

02

Incidents stay at school.

Notes about hitting or grabbing are for teachers and the team only, so a child is not told off twice.

03

Every child gets praised.

The recap flags children nobody has praised yet, so the teacher can whisper a hint before Pamitan Becik.

04

There is a paper version.

Families without a phone or mobile data get a printed Kabar Kampung with the Layang Misi card.

Theory

The ideas behind each tradition.

The same theories as the intervention design. Kabar Kampung adds no new theory; it only extends their reach into the home.

01

Process model of emotion regulation

James Gross
Situation→Attention→Response

Panggonan Tenang (the calm place) changes the situation, shifts attention, and calms the body.

02

Emotion coaching

John Gottman
Notice→Name the feeling→Guide

Naming the feeling comes first. Swara Rasa (voice of feelings) trains teachers and parents to do it.

03

Social learning

Albert Bandura
Adults model→Children copy

Children learn from adults they trust, and from friends they see being praised.

04

Co-regulation

Lev Vygotsky
Guided→Reminded→On their own

The stages teachers log follow support that slowly fades over time.

05

Ecological systems

Urie Bronfenbrenner
School↔Home

Kabar Kampung strengthens the link between school and home, where children spend most of their time.

+

Ing ngarsa sung tuladha“the one in front sets the example”

Ki Hajar Dewantara

The teacher, as pamong (guiding mentor), leads by example. Kabar Kampung invites parents to be that example at home too.

Evaluation

How we will know it works.

Pre- and post-tests still use the three instruments from the design. Kabar Kampung data is only used to monitor how the programme is running.

0–28

Emotion recognition test

A trained observer, about 10 minutes per child: faces, situations, and responses.

10 × 0–3

Behaviour observation checklist

From adult-assisted to independent, during class activities, break time, and transitions.

1–5

Parent and teacher questionnaire

Rates the last two weeks, then cross-checked to limit bias.

What Kabar Kampung adds to the During stage: the Layang Misi card return rate, the calm-down log, and how praise is spread, all available without manual tallying.
